PEER REVIEWED & PREPRINTS


Knowledge and Compliance with Standard Precautions for Nosocomial Infection Prevention among Undergraduate Nursing and Midwifery Students at a Ghanaian University.
(PDF)
medRxiv

Catherine Tenewaa Osei, Agnes Opoku Asare, Yvonne Oti Agyen, Hilda Adwubi Osei, Philemon A Amooba

A cross-sectional study of 150 third-year nursing and midwifery students at KNUST, Ghana, examining knowledge of healthcare-associated infections and compliance with WHO/CDC-aligned standard precautions. Found high knowledge (95.3% of respondents) but a gap in translating that knowledge into consistent practice, compliance was strong for hand hygiene and glove use but notably weaker for PPE use during splash-risk procedures and safe needle handling, with no statistically significant link found between knowledge level and compliance level.

ATONSU-WASTE-PROJECT

A machine learning study connecting improper waste disposal behavior to health outcomes in Atonsu, Kumasi, combining a Random Forest model trained on real field survey data (macro F1 = 0.70) with a MobileNetV2 image classifier for automated waste sorting (88.2% accuracy). Findings from this project are being developed into a research preprint, extending original 2022 fieldwork into a full ML-driven public health study.

Skills used:Python, TensorFlow/Keras, Scikit-Learn, Random Forest, Transfer Learning (MobileNetV2), Field Survey Data Analysis, LaTeX
