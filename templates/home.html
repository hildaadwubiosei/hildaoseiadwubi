Waste Disposal & Health Outcomes: A Machine Learning Study in Atonsu, Kumasi

A field-data-driven study combining a Random Forest model predicting illness risk from waste disposal behavior (macro F1 = 0.70) with a MobileNetV2 image classifier for automated waste sorting (88% accuracy, macro F1 = 0.87). Built on real survey data from 8 sub-areas of Atonsu, Ghana, this work bridges public health and applied ML for waste management in emerging economies.

FarmDirect: ML-Driven Agricultural Marketplace Infrastructure

Building a farmer-to-buyer marketplace that eliminates middlemen using regression and clustering models for price optimization (22% RMSE improvement), validated through a 75-farmer pilot testing text vs. voice interfaces in Twi and English. Now expanding into supply chain and environmental data integration in collaboration with the Ghana Atomic Energy Commission.
