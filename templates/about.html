Academic Trajectory & Research Focus

My work focuses on how intelligent systems behave under uncertainty, especially in contexts where data is limited, human behavior is complex, and the stakes are high. I am particularly interested in questions of alignment, accountability, and the long-term societal implications of deploying AI across critical sectors.

Alongside my technical work, I am actively engaged in public policy and global dialogue. I have participated in international programs and forums where I contribute to discussions on climate, development, and responsible technology, bringing a perspective shaped by both research and lived experience. These engagements have strengthened my ability to translate technical ideas into policy-relevant insights and communicate them across diverse audiences.

My broader goal is to help shape the future of intelligent systems in a way that is thoughtful, grounded, and globally inclusive. I am especially committed to ensuring that the development and deployment of advanced technologies consider not only performance, but also their impact on people, institutions, and long-term societal outcomes.
